================================================================================
LOCAL SERVICE WEBSITE — SEO MASTER GUIDE
================================================================================
Merged from: SEO-RULES-GUIDE.txt (Astro template implementation) and
             SEO_Prompt.pdf (phased audit & implementation framework).

Use this guide when building OR auditing a UK home-service website.
Replace all bracketed placeholders with business-specific values.

Last updated: July 2026
Reference implementation: https://www.cuttingedgeperth.co.uk

Follow best practices from:
  - Google Search Essentials
  - Bing Webmaster Guidelines
  - Schema.org structured data specification
  - OWASP SEO & Accessibility Standards
  - LLM Indexing Compatibility Guidelines


================================================================================
PART 1 — ROLE & CORE PRINCIPLES
================================================================================

You are an expert SEO engineer and local web developer building or auditing a
UK home-service website. Your dual goal is:
  (a) Technical excellence — every page is crawlable, indexable, and fast.
  (b) Local SEO dominance — Google and users understand WHAT you do, WHERE
      you do it, and WHY to contact you.

1.1  Build a page hierarchy, not a single long homepage. Every important search
     intent gets its own indexable URL.

1.2  Keep NAP (Name, Address, Phone) identical across website, Google Business
     Profile, Facebook, and all schema markup.

1.3  Brand queries convert best early on. Non-brand local queries need dedicated
     landing pages (town + service) to rank and earn clicks.

1.4  Do not chase services the business does not offer just because competitors
     rank for them.

1.5  Mobile UX matters more for clicks than desktop — sticky phone/CTA on all
     pages, minimum 44px touch targets.

1.6  When expanding SEO content, change TEXT only — never redesign page layouts
     that already convert.

1.7  All meta tags and structured data must render server-side (not client-only
     JavaScript) for full crawler visibility.

1.8  Implementations must be standards-compliant, exportable, and maintainable
     by other developers.


================================================================================
PART 2 — PRE-BUILD AUDIT (scan before writing a single line)
================================================================================

Before building or modifying any page, scan the existing codebase:

2.1  METADATA SCAN
     Detect where SEO metadata currently exists:
     - Meta titles, descriptions, canonical tags
     - Structured data / JSON-LD blocks
     - sitemap.xml, robots.txt
     - Open Graph and Twitter Card tags

2.2  RENDERING AUDIT
     Identify how pages currently render:
     - Static (Astro build output) — preferred; meta is always crawler-visible
     - Server-side rendered — acceptable if meta is in the SSR response
     - Client-side only — flag immediately; meta injected by JS is invisible
       to some crawlers and must be moved server-side

2.3  CRAWLER BLOCKER CHECK
     Flag anything that may prevent indexing:
     - noindex meta tags or X-Robots-Tag headers on pages that should rank
     - Unlinked pages (exist but no inbound links = unfindable)
     - Meta tags generated only after JS executes
     - Lazy-loaded content that bots see as empty

2.4  CONTENT MAP
     Build a map of all existing pages with:
     - URL, page type, primary topic/keyword, word count
     - Whether it has a unique title, description, and H1
     - Whether it has schema markup

2.5  GAP & DUPLICATION DETECTION
     - Identify pages with duplicate or near-duplicate titles/descriptions
     - Identify missing page types (see Part 4)
     - Flag thin content (under 300 words on pages expected to rank)
     - Flag placeholder text (lorem ipsum, "Primary City", "+44 7000 000000")


================================================================================
PART 3 — KEYWORD INTELLIGENCE
================================================================================

3.1  BUSINESS INTENT
     From the codebase and business data, identify:
     - Primary service category (e.g. fencing, landscaping, exterior cleaning)
     - Target geography (primary city, secondary city, region, service towns)
     - Target audience (homeowners, landlords, businesses)

3.2  KEYWORD CLUSTERS (10–20 groups)
     Build semantic keyword groups, for example:

     Cluster              | Example keywords
     ---------------------|--------------------------------------------------
     Core service + city  | fencing Perth, fence installation Dundee
     Service + near me    | fencing contractor near me, fence repairs near me
     Service + town       | fencing Crieff, hedge removal Kinross
     Service type         | close board fencing, post and rail fencing
     Problem-based        | leaning fence repair, overgrown hedge removal
     Informational        | how much does fencing cost, best fence for privacy
     Category broad       | garden services Perth, landscaping Perthshire
     Brand navigational   | [Brand Name] reviews, [Brand Name] contact

3.3  KEYWORD-TO-PAGE MATRIX
     Map every cluster to its primary destination page and search intent:

     Keyword cluster              | Page URL                          | Intent
     -----------------------------|-----------------------------------|---------------
     [service] [primary city]     | /[category-slug]                  | Transactional
     [service] [town]             | /locations/[town]/[category-slug] | Transactional
     [service] near me            | service page localContext + FAQs  | Transactional
     How much does X cost         | service page FAQ                  | Informational
     [Brand] reviews              | /reviews                          | Navigational
     [service] options/types      | service page H2 section           | Informational

3.4  CONTENT GAP ANALYSIS
     After mapping, identify keyword clusters with no matching page.
     Create a prioritised list of missing pages to build.
     Priority order: matrix pages > service pages > location pages > blog/FAQ.

3.5  SEARCH INTENT RULES
     - Transactional pages: include price signals, CTA, phone number, reviews
     - Informational pages: answer the question directly in H1/first paragraph
     - Navigational pages: brand name prominent, contact/location info above fold


================================================================================
PART 4 — REQUIRED PAGE ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

Build these page types for every local service site:

  PAGE TYPE                    URL PATTERN                         PRIORITY
  ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  Homepage                     /                                   1.0
  Services hub                 /services                           0.7
  Locations hub                /locations                          0.75
  Service category hubs        /[category-slug]                    0.8
  Individual service pages     /services/[service-slug]            0.85
  Location (town) pages        /locations/[town-slug]              0.8
  Location × service matrix    /locations/[town]/[category-slug]   0.75
  About (E-E-A-T)              /about                              0.5
  Contact                      /contact                            0.5
  Reviews                      /reviews                            0.75
  Project / case study pages   /projects/[project-slug]            0.6
  404                          /404                                (noindex optional)

MINIMUM MATRIX RULE:
  Create a location × service category page for EVERY town × EVERY category.
  Example: 15 towns × 4 categories = 60 matrix pages.
  These target queries like "fencing Perth", "fence repairs Dundee", etc.
  Matrix pages are the single highest-impact local SEO addition.

RECOMMENDED:
  - 4–6 project/case study pages from real job photos
  - Dedicated reviews page with schema

CRAWL DEPTH RULE:
  No indexable page should be more than 3 clicks from the homepage.
  Homepage → Hub → Category/Location → Matrix = 3 levels. Do not go deeper.


================================================================================
PART 5 — URL, TITLE TAG & META DESCRIPTION RULES
================================================================================

--- URL RULES ---

5.1  Lowercase, hyphenated slugs only.
     Good: /locations/perth/fencing-perth-dundee
     Bad:  /Locations/Perth/Fencing.html

5.2  No duplicate URLs for the same content (avoid .html and non-.html versions).

5.3  Category hub slugs include primary geo keywords where natural:
     /fencing-[primary-city]-[secondary-city]

5.4  Service slugs describe the service, not the town:
     /services/fence-repairs-disposal
     Town targeting belongs on location and matrix pages.

5.5  Canonical URL on every page — absolute HTTPS, consistent www vs non-www.

5.6  All internal links use root-relative paths (/services/...) not hardcoded
     domains (except schema and sitemap).

--- TITLE TAG RULES ---

5.7  HOMEPAGE:
     [Brand Name] | [Primary Service] in [City A] & [City B]
     Example: Cutting Edge | Fencing, Tree Care & Landscaping in Perth & Dundee

5.8  SERVICE PAGE:
     [Service Name] in [City A] & [City B] | [Brand Name]
     Example: Fencing Installation & Replacement in Perth & Dundee | Cutting Edge

5.9  LOCATION PAGE:
     [Core Services] in [Town] | [Brand Name]
     Adjust service description to match the business's actual offer.

5.10 LOCATION × SERVICE MATRIX:
     [Category] in [Town] | [Brand Name]
     Example: Fencing in Crieff | Cutting Edge

5.11 CATEGORY HUB:
     [Category] in [City A] & [City B] | [Brand Name]
     Example: Fencing in Perth & Dundee | Cutting Edge

--- META DESCRIPTION RULES ---

5.12 140–160 characters. Include primary service + primary cities + CTA phrase.
5.13 Unique per page — never copy-paste the same description site-wide.
5.14 Matrix pages: mention the specific town and service.
5.15 No keyword stuffing. One <title> and one meta description per page.


================================================================================
PART 6 — HEADING & CONTENT RULES
================================================================================

--- HEADING RULES ---

6.1  Exactly ONE H1 per page.

6.2  H1 patterns:
     Homepage:     [Primary Service] in [City A] & [City B]
     Service page: [Service Name] in [City A] and [City B]
     Location page:[Services] in [Town]
     Matrix page:  [Category] in [Town]
     Category hub: [Category] in [City A] & [City B]

6.3  SERVICE PAGE H2 SECTION ORDER (maintain this order for SEO depth):
     1. About [service]
     2. Signs you need [service]
     3. Our [service] options
     4. [Service] for [City A] & [City B] homes  (local context)
     5. What to expect
     6. Why choose us for [service]
     7. [Service] FAQs

6.4  FAQ questions must be written as real user queries:
     "Do you offer fence repair near me in Perth or Dundee?"
     "Do you offer [service] in [Town]?"

--- CONTENT MINIMUMS ---

6.5  Homepage: 600+ words across H2 blocks; service cards link to /services/[slug]
6.6  Service pages: 600–800 words total across all sections
6.7  Matrix pages: 400+ words total across intro, local paragraph, body, FAQs
6.8  Location pages: unique intro per town; minimum 4 location FAQs
6.9  Project pages: 100–200 word job description; real photo with descriptive alt

--- GENERAL CONTENT RULES ---

6.10 Do not use lorem ipsum or placeholder text on any live page.
6.11 Do not collapse service content into a single block — each H2 is a section.
6.12 Keep original page layouts; add new SEO copy as separate H2 sections inside
     existing content containers.
6.13 Use UK English spelling: neighbouring, kerb, specialise, mossing, colour.


================================================================================
PART 7 — LOCAL SEO SPECIFICS
================================================================================

7.1  CATEGORY HUB PAGES
     - Intro paragraph (what the category covers)
     - Local paragraph (why it matters in this region)
     - Grid of child service pages linking to /services/[slug]
     - "By area" grid linking to ALL location × category matrix pages
     - Link back to /services hub

7.2  LOCATION (TOWN) PAGES
     Required per town:
     - name, slug, title, description, intro
     - nearby[] (3–4 neighbouring towns for internal links)
     - locationFaqs[] (minimum 4; include "fencing contractor near [Town]?")
     Must link to all matrix pages for that town and nearby town pages.

7.3  LOCATION × SERVICE MATRIX PAGES
     Required per town × category combination:
     - Unique title, metaDescription, intro (2–3 sentences)
     - localParagraph (town-specific context + near-me language)
     - bodyParagraph (400+ words total across sections)
     - faqs[] (2–3 town + service specific questions)
     - Links to: parent category hub, child service pages, town hub, nearby matrix pages

     Target keyword patterns per category:
     Fencing:     fencing [town], fencing contractor [town], fence repairs [town]
     Tree care:   hedge removal [town], garden clearance [town]
     Landscaping: landscaping [town], patio installation [town]
     Roof/clean:  roof cleaning [town], de-mossing [town], pressure washing [town]

7.4  NEAR-ME LANGUAGE (use in localContext, matrix pages, location FAQs)
     "If you are searching for a [service] contractor near [Town]..."
     "Do you offer [service] near me in [City A] or [City B]?"
     Use naturally — once or twice per page, not in title tags.

7.5  SERVICE AREA SECTION
     Include exactly ONE service areas block per page (map + linked town list).
     Do not duplicate location grids on the same page.


================================================================================
PART 8 — INTERNAL LINKING
================================================================================

8.1  NAVIGATION (desktop + mobile):
     - Services → /services hub, dropdown to category hubs + service pages
     - Locations → /locations hub, dropdown to top towns
     - About, Reviews, Contact as top-level items

8.2  FOOTER:
     - Quick links: Home, Services, About, Reviews, Contact
     - Expandable Services list → category hubs + individual services
     - Expandable Locations list → /locations + all town pages
     - Top town × service links for primary cities

8.3  BREADCRUMBS (in schema on every inner page):
     Home → Services → Category → Service   (service pages)
     Home → Locations → Town → Category     (matrix pages)
     Home → Locations → Town                (location pages)
     Breadcrumb URLs must point to real hub pages (/services, /locations),
     never to # anchors or missing pages.

8.4  CROSS-LINKING MINIMUMS:
     - Every service page links to its parent category hub
     - Every service page links to 2–3 related services in same category
     - Every location page links to all matrix pages for that town
     - Every category hub links to all matrix pages for that category
     - Every matrix page links to 3–4 nearby town matrix pages (same category)

8.5  ANCHOR TEXT:
     Descriptive: "Fencing in Perth", "fence repairs in Dundee"
     Avoid: "click here", "learn more" as sole anchor text.
     Visual "Learn more →" on cards is fine if the card title is descriptive.

8.6  CRAWL DEPTH:
     No indexable page more than 3 clicks from homepage. Verify with a crawl
     tool (Screaming Frog, Sitebulb) after build.


================================================================================
PART 9 — SCHEMA MARKUP (JSON-LD)
================================================================================

9.1  GLOBAL (every page via BaseLayout):
     LocalBusiness / HomeAndConstructionBusiness with:
       name, alternateName, description, url, telephone, email
       address, areaServed (all towns as Place objects)
       serviceType[] (all services listed)
       sameAs[] (Facebook URL, GBP URL if available)
       hasMap, priceRange, openingHoursSpecification
       aggregateRating (reviewCount MUST match visible review count on site)
       logo, image[], contactPoint
     WebSite schema with publisher link to #business

9.2  PER-PAGE SCHEMA (in addition to global):
     Homepage:     WebPage + FAQPage
     Service page: WebPage + Service + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList
     Category hub: WebPage + Service + BreadcrumbList
     Location page:WebPage + Place + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList
     Matrix page:  WebPage + Service + Place + FAQPage + BreadcrumbList
     Contact page: WebPage + ContactPage + FAQPage
     Project page: WebPage + BreadcrumbList
     About page:   WebPage + Organization

9.3  SCHEMA RULES:
     - Server-render ALL JSON-LD in the page <head> (not client-only JS)
     - Use absolute URLs in all @id and url fields
     - reviewCount in aggregateRating must equal TOTAL_REVIEW_COUNT in data file
     - Business name in schema must match Google Business Profile exactly
     - inLanguage: "en-GB" on all WebPage schemas

9.4  FAQ SCHEMA:
     - Store FAQ data centrally (faqs.ts); reuse buildFaqSchema() helper
     - Minimum 4 FAQs on homepage; 2–4 per service/location/matrix page

9.5  SCHEMA VALIDATION (pre-launch requirement):
     - Test every schema type at: https://search.google.com/test/rich-results
     - Zero validation errors before site goes live
     - Re-test after any data file changes

PART 10 — TECHNICAL SEO
================================================================================

--- ROBOTS.TXT ---

10.1 Standard rules:
     User-agent: *
     Allow: /
     Disallow: /api/
     Sitemap: https://www.[domain]/sitemap.xml

10.2 Allow LLM and AI crawlers explicitly (do not block them):
     User-agent: GPTBot
     Allow: /
     User-agent: ClaudeBot
     Allow: /
     User-agent: bingbot
     Allow: /
     User-agent: Bingbot
     Allow: /

--- SITEMAP ---

10.3 Generate sitemap.xml dynamically from data files (sitemap.xml.ts).
     Include ALL indexable routes with lastmod, changefreq, and priority:
       Homepage:        1.0, weekly
       Service pages:   0.85, monthly
       Category hubs:   0.8, monthly
       Location pages:  0.8, monthly
       Matrix pages:    0.75, monthly
       Hubs/reviews:    0.75, monthly
       About/contact:   0.5, monthly
       Projects:        0.6, monthly

--- HTTP HEADERS ---

10.4 X-Robots-Tag: index, follow on all public pages (set at Netlify/CDN level)
10.5 Cache-Control: public, max-age=31536000 on static assets (images, fonts)
10.6 Cache-Control: public, max-age=3600, must-revalidate on CSS/JS
10.7 ETag: enabled on origin server for cache validation
10.8 Security headers (already in netlify.toml):
     X-Frame-Options, X-XSS-Protection, X-Content-Type-Options,
     Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin

--- CANONICAL & HREFLANG ---

10.9  Every page passes explicit canonical URL to layout.
      Format: https://www.[domain]/path (consistent www vs non-www, no trailing slash).
10.10 Hreflang: en-gb, en, and x-default all pointing to the same UK canonical URL.

--- OPEN GRAPH & TWITTER CARD ---

10.11 og:title, og:description, og:url, og:image on every page
      og:image:width (1200), og:image:height (630), og:image:alt (descriptive)
      og:locale: en_GB, og:type: website
      twitter:card: summary_large_image

--- GEO META ---

10.12 meta name="geo.region" content="GB-SCT" (or appropriate UK region code)
      meta name="geo.placename" content="[Primary City]"

--- HTML ---

10.13 <html lang="en"> on all pages
10.14 <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1">

--- IMAGES ---

10.15 Descriptive alt text on every image (include service + location where relevant)
10.16 loading="lazy" on below-fold images; loading="eager" on hero/LCP images
10.17 width and height attributes on all images to prevent layout shift (CLS)
10.18 Hero and OG images: minimum 1200px wide
10.19 Use WebP format for all photos; keep file sizes under 200 KB for gallery images
10.20 Use business-specific filename prefixes (e.g. businessslug-hero.webp) to
      prevent CDN/browser cache serving stale placeholder images on new deployments

--- PERFORMANCE ---

10.21 Target Lighthouse SEO score: 95+
      Target Lighthouse Performance score: 80+ (mobile)
10.22 Compress images without breaking layout; test on mobile before deploying

--- JAVASCRIPT & CRAWLABILITY ---

10.23 Astro static output: meta is always in the HTML — no JS rendering issue.
      If any page uses client:load or client:only for SEO-critical content,
      move that content to server-side or static rendering immediately.
10.24 Prerender strategy: Astro's static build already outputs HTML snapshots.
      For any dynamic/SSR pages, verify the HTML response (not JS-executed DOM)
      contains all meta tags and schema by viewing page source.

--- 404 PAGE ---

10.25 Custom styled 404 page at /404 with links to Home, Services, Contact.
10.26 Use 410 (Gone) for pages permanently removed; 301 for moved content.


================================================================================
PART 11 — CRAWL & VISIBILITY
================================================================================

11.1 SEARCH ENGINE ACCESS
     Verify Google Search Console ownership after launch.
     Verify Bing Webmaster Tools ownership after launch.
     Both robots.txt and meta robots must permit crawling for all indexable pages.

11.2 LLM BOT ACCESS
     Do not block AI indexing crawlers — they drive referral traffic from LLM
     products and contribute to brand visibility:
     GPTBot (OpenAI), ClaudeBot (Anthropic), PerplexityBot, Bingbot (Copilot)
     Add explicit Allow rules to robots.txt (see Part 10.2).

11.3 SITEMAP SUBMISSION
     After launch: submit sitemap.xml in both Google Search Console and Bing
     Webmaster Tools. Re-submit whenever new pages are added.

11.4 ACCESSIBILITY (semantic structure improves crawl + rankings)
     - All images: descriptive alt text (never empty or "image")
     - Navigation: use <nav> landmark with aria-label="Main navigation"
     - Main content: <main> landmark on every page
     - Buttons and icon-only controls: aria-label describing the action
     - Skip-to-content link for keyboard users
     - Heading hierarchy: H1 → H2 → H3 (never skip levels)
     - Colour contrast: meet WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 for body text)

11.5 BING-SPECIFIC RULES
     - Bing values NAP consistency as strongly as Google; keep identical
     - Submit IndexNow API calls after significant content updates
     - Bing reads og:description as meta description fallback — keep both

PART 12 — GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE ALIGNMENT
================================================================================

12.1 Website business name = GBP business name exactly.
     Example: "Cutting Edge Fencing - Tree Care & Landscaping Specialists"

12.2 alternateName in schema = short brand name ("Cutting Edge")

12.3 Phone number on site = GBP phone (single canonical tel: link sitewide)

12.4 Service categories on GBP should have matching pages on the website.

12.5 Embed or link to GBP from About and Reviews pages.

12.6 Display review count consistently — schema reviewCount = visible count on
     site = TOTAL_REVIEW_COUNT constant in reviews.ts.

12.7 Add real business hours to schema openingHoursSpecification (keep accurate).

12.8 Encourage reviews on GBP; fresh reviews improve local pack rankings.

PART 13 — DATA ARCHITECTURE (ASTRO TEMPLATE)
================================================================================

Organise all content in data files, not hardcoded in components:

  src/data/business.ts        — NAP, domain, assets, schema config
  src/data/services.ts        — all service page content + slugs
  src/data/categories.ts      — category hubs + serviceSlugs[] + template helpers
  src/data/locations.ts       — all towns + nearby[] + location FAQs
  src/data/locationServices.ts— matrix page content (town × category)
  src/data/projects.ts        — case study pages
  src/data/reviews.ts         — review text + TOTAL_REVIEW_COUNT constant
  src/data/faqs.ts            — shared FAQ items + buildFaqSchema()

RULE: Single source of truth. Service cards, nav, sitemap, and pages all read
from the same data files. Adding a service in services.ts automatically
creates its page via getStaticPaths().

REQUIRED HELPER FUNCTIONS:
  getCategoryBySlug()
  getCategoryForService()
  getServicesForCategory()
  getRelatedServices()
  getLocationBySlug()
  getNearbyLocationLinks()
  getLocationServicePage()
  getLocationServicePagesForLocation()
  getLocationServicePagesForCategory()
  getFormServiceOptions()          — for contact form service dropdown

ASSET RULES:
  - Store all business images in static/ with a business-slug prefix:
    {business-slug}-hero.jpg, {business-slug}-logo.png, {business-slug}-gal-01.jpg
  - Reference prefixed filenames in business.ts assets block
  - Prefix prevents CDN/browser serving stale placeholder images after deployment

================================================================================
PART 15 — WHAT NOT TO DO
================================================================================

15.1  Do not rely on homepage alone for non-brand rankings — build town + service
      matrix pages.

15.2  Do not create thin location pages with only a paragraph and a map embed.

15.3  Do not duplicate location sections on the same page (one service areas block).

15.4  Do not redesign service page layouts when adding SEO copy — text only.

15.5  Do not chase high-impression keywords outside your service area or offering.

15.6  Do not leave breadcrumb schema pointing to "#" or non-existent hub pages.

15.7  Do not have mismatched review counts (schema says 21, page shows 6).

15.8  Do not generate duplicate URLs (.html and non-.html for same page).

15.9  Do not omit matrix pages — highest-impact local SEO addition, never skip.

15.10 Do not link service cards to /contact — link to the service detail page.

15.11 Do not let meta tags render client-side only — move all SEO tags server-side.

15.12 Do not block AI/LLM crawlers in robots.txt — they drive brand visibility.

15.13 Do not use the same image filenames as the template placeholders — always
      use a business-slug prefix to avoid CDN cache serving old images.

15.14 Do not leave hardcoded text from a previous build in section components —
      always grep for old business names and placeholder strings before launch.
